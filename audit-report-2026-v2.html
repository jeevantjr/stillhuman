Section 04

Medical Ethics & Government Compliance

68 / 100

As a government-paid Medical Officer and MD Health Informatics Trainee subject to the Sri Lanka Establishments Code, the website must not use government designation, hospital name, or position for commercial purposes; must not advertise paid external contracts; and must maintain a clear distinction between personal academic identity and government service role.

Issues Found

Critical "Health Informatician" as title — live on three pages cv.html · portfolio.html · ai-health-advice.html

The live CV subtitle reads "General Practitioner · Health Informatician · Author · Historical Researcher". The live portfolio footer reads "MD Health Informatics Trainee · AI Reviewer · Health Informatician · Digital Health Developer · Physician". The ai-health-advice meta description reads "A physician and health informatician reflects…". "Health Informatician" is not an approved professional designation under SLMC or Ministry frameworks. Correct term: Health Informatics Researcher or MD Health Informatics Trainee.

✅ cv.html subtitle fixed locally. Push cv.html. Also fix portfolio.html footer and ai-health-advice meta description.
Critical Supervisor names and Ministry positions publicly listed on CV cv.html

The live CV page publicly names: Dr. Anuradha Jayathilake (PD Office), Dr. Sridharan Sathasivam, DDG Planning, Dr. Palitha Karunapema, CCP, Dr. Kaushalya Kasturiarachchi, CCP. These are named government officers with designated roles. Publishing their full names, job titles, and supervisory relationship on a public website they may not have consented to appear on carries professional risk. If this CV is reviewed by SLMC or Ministry supervisors, it could create friction.

Remove supervisor names from the public CV. Keep rotation locations and dates. Supervisor details can be provided upon direct request in a private application context.
⚠ This is the most sensitive ethics issue on the site. The current local cv.html still contains these names.
Critical Commercial solicitation on portfolio page portfolio.html

The live portfolio footer section reads: "Open to AI reviewer roles, health informatics consulting, digital health development projects, and research collaborations. Let's build something meaningful." — explicit commercial solicitation of paid external contracts during a period of government employment. This is prohibited under the Establishments Code.

Replace with: "Open to academic correspondence, research discussion, and professional networking." — the standard framing used on the main site.
High "Historical Researcher" in CV subtitle cv.html

Live CV subtitle includes "Historical Researcher" — this is not an appropriate professional designation for a medical CV that may be reviewed by PGIM, SLMC, or Ministry officials. Tamil historical scholarship belongs in the Publications section of the CV, not as a professional designation.

✅ Fixed locally: subtitle now reads "General Practitioner · MD Health Informatics Trainee · Health Informatics Researcher · Author". Push cv.html.
High "Available for remote collaboration in digital health research, DHIS2 implementation, and clinical AI review" about.html

The live about.html bio card contains this sentence. In context it reads as a service advertisement for paid work — a GP, DHIS2 implementation, and AI review are all fee-earning services. Under the Establishments Code, advertising availability for paid external work while employed as a government MO is prohibited.

Change to: "Open to academic correspondence, research discussion, and professional networking." (This has already been done in the local about.html — push the file.)

Section 07

User Experience & Accessibility

58 / 100

Navigation Experience

The largest UX failure on the site is navigation inconsistency. A user navigating from the homepage to About to CV to Portfolio encounters four completely different navigation structures. This creates confusion about the boundaries of the site and erodes trust in professionalism.

Mobile Experience

High Inner page text smaller than article pages on mobile

Article pages set html { font-size: 18px; } globally. Main inner pages (about, cv, notes, research, portfolio, books) rely on style.css which — on the live site — only sets the base font size in the mobile breakpoint, not globally. As a result, all rem-sized elements on inner pages render ~11% smaller than on article pages. Readers moving from an article to the CV page notice an immediate size drop.

✅ Fixed in local style.css: html { font-size: 18px; } added to the global html rule. Push style.css.

Counter Animation Issue

Low Stats counters show "0" before JavaScript loads index.html · portfolio.html

Both the homepage and portfolio page use JavaScript-animated count-up numbers (e.g. "20+ Years", "2 Publications"). Before JS loads, all counters display "0". On slower connections, users briefly see a row of zeros which looks like broken content.

Set the initial HTML value to the final number (e.g. 20) and animate up from a lower value, or provide a noscript fallback.
