Peer-Reviewed Publications
Original Research · BMJ Open · 2020 · PMID: 32690534

Imputing HbA1c from capillary blood glucose levels in patients with type 2 diabetes in Sri Lanka: a cross-sectional study

Choo M, Hoy GE, Dugan SP, McEwen LN, Gunaratnam N, Wyckoff J, Jeevaraaj T, Saththiyaseelan A, Ganeikabahu B, Katulanda P, Balis U, Herman WH, Saha AK.

BMJ Open · July 2020 · doi: 10.1136/bmjopen-2020-038148 · Open Access

International multi-centre collaboration with the University of Michigan, Division of Metabolism, Endocrinology & Diabetes. Field data collected in Sri Lanka by Dr Jeevaraaj. Investigated whether HbA1c values can be reliably derived from point-of-care capillary blood glucose readings in a resource-constrained South Asian diabetes population. Findings have implications for NCD management in low-resource clinical settings where HbA1c testing is not routinely available.

Multi-Centre Research · Jaffna Medical Journal · December 2024

The Correlation Between Global Longitudinal Strain by Speckle Tracking Echocardiography and Coronary Angiogram Finding in Patients with Suspected Chronic Stable Angina Pectoris

Jeyakanth T, Mayurathan G, Kularatne A, Jeevaraaj T, Prashanthi J, Abeysingha T.

Jaffna Medical Journal · Volume 36, Issue 2 · December 2024 · doi: 10.4038/jmj.v36i2.221

Multi-centre clinical study across Teaching Hospital Kandy, District General Hospital Trincomalee (Dr Jeevaraaj), and Teaching Hospital Jaffna. Evaluated whether Global Longitudinal Strain (GLS) by speckle-tracking echocardiography predicts significant coronary artery disease in 78 patients with chronic stable angina and normal LV function. GLS cutoff of −19 demonstrated clinically useful diagnostic performance for CAD detection.

Research Interests
Health Information Systems in Low-Resource Settings

DHIS2 implementation, HIS governance, and what actually happens when national-level HIS design meets peripheral health reality. Gap analysis between design intention and frontline practice.

Clinical AI Governance & Evaluation

AI implementation in clinical contexts, physician-AI interaction, hallucination detection in medical AI, and governance frameworks for AI in South Asian healthcare.

Peripheral & Primary Care Informatics

Digital health in post-conflict, resource-constrained, and geographically remote settings. NCD surveillance, disease reporting, and data quality in peripheral contexts.

Implementation Science in Digital Health

Why digital health programmes succeed or fail at scale. Participatory design, user adoption, behaviour change, and the human factors that determine HIS outcomes.
