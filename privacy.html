About this website

This is a personal academic website maintained by Dr. T. Jeevaraj (MBBS, MCGP, MSc Biomedical Informatics, MD Health Informatics Trainee). It is not a commercial website. No goods or services are sold here. The site exists to share research, writing, and professional information in an academic and personal capacity.

All content reflects the personal views of the author. It does not represent the views, positions, or endorsements of any affiliated institution, employer, or organization.

What data is collected

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with the site. GA4 may collect:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Approximate geographic location (country and city level, derived from IP address)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet) and browser
  • Referral source (how you arrived at the site — e.g., search engine, direct link)
  • Aggregate visitor counts

IP addresses are anonymised before processing. No personally identifiable information is collected. This website does not use any forms, user accounts, or comment systems that collect your name, email, or other personal data.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish returning visitors and track session activity. These are first-party analytics cookies stored in your browser. No advertising or tracking cookies from third parties are used.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You may also opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

How data is used

Analytics data is used only to understand which content is helpful to readers — for example, which articles are read, where visitors come from, and whether the site works properly on mobile devices. It informs improvements to the site's content and structure.

No data is sold, shared with third parties for commercial purposes, or used for advertising targeting.

Third-party services

This website uses the following third-party services that may set their own cookies or collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics 4 — website analytics. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — typography served from Google's CDN. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Font Awesome (Cloudflare CDN) — icons. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting. See GitHub Privacy Statement.

External links to ORCID, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, LinkedIn, and Amazon are provided for reference. Visiting these sites is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Your rights

Because this website does not collect personally identifiable information directly, there is no personal data stored about you that can be retrieved or deleted upon request. For data held by Google Analytics, you may use the Google Analytics opt-out tool.
