About this website

This is a personal academic website maintained by Dr. T. Jeevaraj (MBBS, MCGP, MSc Biomedical Informatics, MD Health Informatics Trainee). It is not a commercial website. No goods or services are sold here. The site exists to share research, writing, and professional information in an academic and personal capacity.

All content reflects the personal views of the author and does not represent the views, positions, or endorsements of any affiliated institution, employer, or organization.

What data is collected

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with the site. GA4 may collect:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level, derived from anonymised IP)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet) and browser
  • Referral source — how you arrived at the site
  • Aggregate visitor counts

IP addresses are anonymised before processing. No personally identifiable information is collected. This website has no forms, user accounts, or comment systems that collect your name, email, or any personal data.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish returning visitors and track session activity. These are first-party analytics cookies stored in your browser. No advertising or tracking cookies from third parties are used.

Analytics data is used only to understand which content is helpful to readers — which articles are read, where visitors come from, and whether the site works properly on mobile devices. It informs improvements to the site's content and structure.

  • Data is never shared for commercial purposes
