# ARE YOU STILL HUMAN? ### Three true stories, ten patterns, and one quiet choice **By Dr. T. Jeevaraj** What happens when the habits we learn from technology begin to shape the way we live, think, and relate to one another? This short literary work explores that question through three true stories drawn from ordinary life. A software engineer turns an unexpected meeting with an old friend into a scheduling calculation. A father driving home with his teenage son hears eight words that stop him in his tracks: *"Appa, you are prompting me to get your desired answer."* A senior accountant discovers that even grief can be interrupted by a mind trained to optimise, organise, and move forward. None of these stories are about artificial intelligence itself. They are about people. About the subtle ways our daily interactions with technology can influence how we make decisions, process emotions, and understand the people closest to us. Written by a medical doctor and health informatics trainee who works with digital systems every day, *Are You Still Human?* is not a warning against technology. It is a reflection on balance, presence, and the human experiences that cannot be fully automated. A short read of approximately thirty-five minutes. Three stories. Ten patterns.
