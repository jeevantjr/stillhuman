### About

Physician with 20+ years of front-line clinical experience, currently enrolled as an **MD Health Informatics Trainee (Batch 9)** at the Postgraduate Institute of Medicine (PGIM), University of Colombo, completing structured Ministry of Health rotations across Sri Lanka's national health information institutions.

I build open-source digital health tools to fill real gaps I encountered in clinical work — offline-first systems designed for district public health settings where connectivity is unreliable and specialist IT support is unavailable.

Also a researcher, AI reviewer, and author of seven books — including _Are You Still Human?_ (Kindle, 2026), a literary nonfiction book on what artificial intelligence does to ordinary human relationships.
