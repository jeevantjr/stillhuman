Why This Website Exists

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday life. We use it to write, search, plan, analyse and decide. Yet many of the most important parts of life still happen outside algorithms: friendship, grief, family, memory, love and regret.

Still Human explores the place where technology meets ordinary human experience. Not through technical manuals or academic papers, but through stories.

Some are true. Some are imagined. All begin with a simple question: What happens when highly optimised people return home?
