First Impressions

Homepage Strategy

A visitor arriving at drjeevaraj.com has 7 seconds to decide whether to stay. For a WHO programme officer, NHS Digital hiring manager, or AI company recruiter, those 7 seconds must answer three questions: Who is this person? Can they solve my problem? Why should I trust them?

Current Problem
The homepage hero leads with "Still Human" — the book title — as the dominant headline. An international recruiter reads this as a literary site, not a professional one. Most will leave within 5 seconds without understanding your credentials.

The First 5 Seconds Rule

The hero section — the portion visible without scrolling — must communicate your professional identity immediately. The book cover as a visual anchor is excellent; it signals depth and thought leadership. But the text must lead with credentials.

Recommended Hero Headline
Dr. T. Jeevaraj Health Informatician · AI Reviewer · Digital Health Consultant
Medical Doctor · 20+ Years Clinical Experience · BMJ Open · World Bank · Sri Lanka

Three Primary CTAs (above the fold)

🎯

Primary CTA — "See My Work"

Links to portfolio.html. Gold button, most prominent. Answers: "Can you do what I need?"

💼

Secondary CTA — "Work With Me"

Links to services.html. Outlined gold button. For consultants and hiring managers ready to engage.

📄

Tertiary CTA — "Download CV"

Direct PDF download link. For NGO/UN applicants who need a document immediately.

Below-Fold Flow (in order)

After the hero: Trust bar (5 credentials as icons) → What I Do (4 service pillars) → Featured Articles (3 most relevant) → The Book (literary anchor) → Publications & Recognition → Work With Me CTA → Footer.

Key Principle
The book is your differentiator, not your introduction. Show credentials first, then reveal the book as the layer that makes you unforgettable. "A Health Informatician who also wrote a book about AI and humanity" is far more compelling than "An author who also does health informatics."

Friction Points to Remove

Current Friction Points
1. No email address visible without navigating to services page — Put your email in the footer of every page AND in the hero section of the homepage.

2. No LinkedIn button on homepage — LinkedIn is how most international recruiters will reach out. Add a prominent LinkedIn link to the hero and footer.

3. No CV download button on homepage — A "Download CV (PDF)" button in the hero costs nothing and converts visitors who came specifically to assess your qualifications.

4. No clear "what happens next" after reading articles — End every article with a brief author bio and a CTA: "Want to collaborate? See how to work with me →"

LinkedIn as Your Primary Conversion Channel

For international opportunities, LinkedIn is more important than your website for initial discovery. Your website then closes the trust gap. The optimal sequence is: LinkedIn profile finds you → recruiter visits drjeevaraj.com → your site converts them to a contact. Both need to reinforce the same positioning.

LinkedIn Profile Checklist
Headline: "Health Informatician · AI Reviewer · Digital Health Consultant · BMJ Open · World Bank Featured"
