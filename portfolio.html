Imputing HbA1c from capillary blood glucose levels in patients with type 2 diabetes in Sri Lanka: a cross-sectional study

Choo M, Hoy GE, Dugan SP, McEwen LN, Gunaratnam N, Wyckoff J, Jeevaraaj T, Saththiyaseelan A, Ganeikabahu B, Katulanda P, Balis U, Herman WH, Saha AK.

International multi-centre collaboration with the University of Michigan, Division of Metabolism, Endocrinology & Diabetes. Field data collected in Sri Lanka by Dr Jeevaraaj. Jaffna Med J
Original Research · Peer Reviewed · Multi-Centre Study

The Correlation Between Global Longitudinal Strain by Speckle Tracking Echocardiography and Coronary Angiogram Finding in Patients with Suspected Chronic Stable Angina Pectoris

Jeyakanth T, Mayurathan G, Kularatne A, Jeevaraj T, Prashanthi J, Abeysingha T.
Jaffna Medical Journal · Volume 36, Issue 2 · December 2024 · doi:10.4038/jmj.v36i2.221
Multi-centre study across Teaching Hospital Kandy, District General Hospital Trincomalee (Dr Jeevaraaj), and Teaching Hospital Jaffna. Evaluated whether Global Longitudinal Strain (GLS) by speckle-tracking echocardiography predicts significant coronary artery disease in 78 patients with chronic stable angina and normal LV function. GLS cutoff of −19 demonstrated useful diagnostic performance for CAD detection.
