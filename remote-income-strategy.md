## 5. Platforms and Companies — Where to Apply

### Apply immediately (asynchronous, freelance)

**Outlier AI** — outlier.ai/experts/medicine
Your profile (MBBS + MSc + BMJ publication) is a strong fit. Outlier requires a structured onboarding (30–90 minutes) and a qualification test. Medicine experts earn $35–45/hr on standard tasks; informatics-specific tasks push higher. Outlier is owned by Scale AI and operates internationally. Pay is weekly via PayPal or Payoneer.

**DataAnnotation** — dataannotation.tech/medicine
Medical professionals start at $50/hr. The work involves writing clinical responses, evaluating AI answers for accuracy, and fact-checking health content. Apply directly via their medicine track. Accepts international applicants with PayPal or Payoneer payout.

**Alignerr** — alignerr.com
Powered by Labelbox. Pay is $15–60/hr. Easier entry than Outlier. Good for building your first AI review portfolio and track record before applying to higher-paying platforms. Apply via their website.

**TELUS Digital** — telusinternational.com/solutions/ai-data
Baseline $14/hr; medical professionals can qualify for higher-rated tasks. Very accessible. Use as an entry point while building track record.

Pay range $40–120/hr. Specifically recruits practicing physicians. Application requires CV, writing samples, and a skills test. Your combination of ICU experience, informatics, and BMJ publication is exactly what they want. Glassdoor lists active openings for this role.

**Upwork** — upwork.com
Set up a profile as Medical Writer + Health Informatics Specialist. Use your BMJ Open paper as your primary credential. Upload 2–3 articles from Still Human as portfolio samples. Set your rate at $45–65/hr to start; raise after 5 reviews. Medical writing ($30–100/hr), health content review, and clinical informatics consulting are all active categories.

## 9. Brutal Realities You Need to Know

**1. Sri Lanka access is real but variable.** Most platforms (Outlier, DataAnnotation, Invisible Technologies, Surge AI) accept international applicants including Sri Lanka. Payment via PayPal or Payoneer is standard. Verify before investing time in onboarding — payment methods and country availability can change.

**2. Task volume is not guaranteed.** On all annotation platforms, task availability fluctuates. Some weeks there is 30 hours of work; other weeks there is none. You must be on multiple platforms to manage this.

**3. Surge AI rates are real but rare.** The $250–450/hr figure is published and real. But most medical applicants do not get accepted, and accepted applicants do not always find tasks available. Treat it as a high-value option, not a plan.

**4. WHO contracts take time.** Applying today does not mean consulting in 3 months. WHO procurement is slow. Apply early, monitor continuously.

**5. Your MD traineeship is an asset, not a barrier.** Some candidates hide their employment status. Do not. Being an active MD trainee at PGIM, University of Colombo is a credential. It signals institutional affiliation, ongoing clinical exposure, and a structured research context. Lead with it.

**6. Your book is a credential, not a distraction.** "Are You Still Human?" signals that you think seriously about AI and human interaction. AI companies value this perspective. Mention it in every application.

**7. Your informatics is rare.** Among the global pool of doctors applying to these platforms, very few have MSc Biomedical Informatics and active informatics traineeship. This is your strongest differentiator. Never bury it.
