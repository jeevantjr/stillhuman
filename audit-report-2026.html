Section 4

Medical Ethics & E-Code Compliance Review

4.1 Commercial Language Concerns

portfolio.html — Contact section
"Let's Work Together" with commercial service framing
"Open to AI reviewer roles, health informatics consulting, digital health development projects, and research collaborations. Let's build something meaningful."
This is explicit commercial advertising of paid consulting services while the author is a government Medical Officer on paid leave for postgraduate training. The Establishments Code prohibits public officers from engaging in outside employment for remuneration without prior written approval. Publishing a "hire me" section while on government-funded training is a compliance risk.
Revised: "Open to academic correspondence, research collaboration, and professional networking in health informatics and clinical AI."
High
about.html — Identity bio card
"Available for remote collaboration in digital health research, DHIS2 implementation, and clinical AI review"
"Available for remote collaboration in digital health research, DHIS2 implementation, and clinical AI review."
The phrase "available for" has commercial connotations. "DHIS2 implementation" especially sounds like a paid consulting service offer, not academic collaboration.
Revised: "Open to academic correspondence, research discussion, and professional networking."
High
portfolio.html — About section
"Available for remote collaboration worldwide"
"Sri Lanka · Available for remote collaboration worldwide"
Explicitly advertising availability for paid international work.

Section 6

Legal & Compliance Review

6.1 Missing Legal Documents

/ (entire site)
No Privacy Policy
The site uses Google Analytics (GA4), which collects user IP addresses, device data, and browsing behaviour. Under GDPR (applicable to European visitors), a Privacy Policy disclosing this data collection is legally required. Even for visitors from Sri Lanka, a privacy policy is best practice.
Fix: Add a simple Privacy Policy page disclosing GA4 usage. Example disclosure: "This site uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous visitor data. No personally identifiable information is collected. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com."
High
/ (entire site)
No Cookie Consent Banner
Google Analytics sets cookies. GDPR (and many other privacy laws) require informed consent before setting non-essential cookies. Without a cookie banner, the site is non-compliant for EU visitors.
Fix: Add a simple cookie consent mechanism. Since this is a GitHub Pages site, a lightweight JS cookie banner would suffice.
Medium
/ (entire site)
No dedicated Medical Disclaimer page
A footer disclaimer is present but there is no standalone medical disclaimer page. For a site authored by a medical doctor discussing clinical AI, health informatics, and patient encounters, a dedicated disclaimer page is strongly recommended.
Fix: Add a Medical Disclaimer page stating: (a) site content is for informational purposes only; (b) not medical advice; (c) seek qualified medical care for health decisions; (d) no doctor-patient relationship is formed through this website.
Medium

  • ✓ Copyright notice present on all pages: "© 2026 T. Jeevaraj / Dr. T. Jeevaraj".
  • ✓ Disclaimer present in footer: "All content on this website reflects the personal views, analyses, and creative work of the author. It is published in a personal capacity..."
  • ✓ No patient data or clinical records are shared.
  • ✓ No contact form — direct email only (mailto:) — avoids data collection compliance issues.
